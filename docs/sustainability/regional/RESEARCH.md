## Admission decision

Release two products with coarse conditional alternatives, rather than inventing
nationwide route coverage. Apple's regional producer is NY for MA/NY, MI for MI,
WA for WA; conventional alternatives are the other reviewed apple producer states.
Strawberries compare CA and FL in March/April, their documented overlapping
harvest months. No origin is inferred as actual or assigned a probability.

Distances are great-circle miles from a state's Census internal point to the
selected directory market's coordinates. Same-state defines the regional boundary
except the explicit NY–MA region. This is NOT road mileage, a measured farm route,
a distribution-network model or evidence that farmers-market purchases are local.
Conditional bulk-truck CO2e applies the existing average EPA factor to that
straight-line proxy; it is NOT a road-route emissions estimate or a lower bound
on actual emissions (unknown origins, vehicles and load sizes prevent that claim).
Both branches explicitly assume aggregated medium/heavy freight, not farmer vans.

Road-network lanes, actual origin/flow shares, regional consolidation/last-mile
legs and load-specific vehicle evidence remain absent. Do not substitute seasonal
coverage for transport evidence, turn production shares into probabilities, or
claim the geometric proxy is a validated sourcing model. No extra road multiplier,
rail substitution, payload-efficiency multiplier, confidence interval or weighted
mean is invented. Origin alternatives are sensitivity cases, not probabilities.

USDA/Census factual numeric extracts are attributed; no full reports, confidential
farm identities, prose or images are redistributed. No runtime API, paid routing
service, secret, farm coordinate or new dependency is needed.
